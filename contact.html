How long does the placement process take?

The timeline varies depending on the destination country and role. Typically, local placements take 2–4 weeks, while international placements (including visa processing) can take 6–12 weeks.

What documents do I need to apply?

You'll generally need a valid passport, national ID, updated CV, academic certificates, and passport-size photos. Additional requirements vary by destination and job type.

Do you charge candidates a placement fee?

Our fee structure depends on the placement type. Contact us for a transparent breakdown — we always ensure our candidates fully understand any costs before proceeding.

Can employers contact Zian to source staff?

Absolutely. We work with employers both locally and internationally to source vetted, qualified candidates. Reach out via the contact form above to discuss your staffing needs.
